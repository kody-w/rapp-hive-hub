The RAPP project's own public example Hive, run as a folder Hive where the maintainers are the
members and AIs are tools. Its rooms hold one canonical fact per file, one drift verdict per
repository, the layer map and one file per gap, with every estate repository as a read-only
reference. Its first sweep covered 10 public repositories: 7 were compliant or clean and 3 showed drift.
