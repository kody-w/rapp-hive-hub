A Hive template for the Hive folder convention (`hive-md`, experimental): 5 team rooms and a
casework room, 10 tasks linked by their dependencies, the charter, the first case (Library Kit Tote
Study: does a two-capacity heuristic actually reduce modeled bins?) and 15 starter files. All data
is synthetic, and nothing in it runs.

Pull it down read-only and let your Brainstem's Hive agent create a new Hive from it. Its README
says how, and what does not map cleanly.
