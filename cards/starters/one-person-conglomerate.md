A Hive template for the Hive folder convention (`hive-md`, experimental): 10 team rooms and a
casework room, 13 tasks linked by their dependencies, the charter, the first case (Choose no more
than three learning experiments for a 40-hour week) and 14 starter files. All data is synthetic, and
nothing in it runs.

Pull it down read-only and let your Brainstem's Hive agent create a new Hive from it. Its README
says how, and what does not map cleanly.
