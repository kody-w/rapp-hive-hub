A Hive template for the Hive folder convention (`hive-md`, experimental): 6 team rooms and a
casework room, 12 tasks linked by their dependencies, the charter, the first case (Agenda Pocket:
make a 60-minute meeting fit without hiding required topics) and 15 starter files. All data is
synthetic, and nothing in it runs.

Pull it down read-only and let your Brainstem's Hive agent create a new Hive from it. Its README
says how, and what does not map cleanly.
