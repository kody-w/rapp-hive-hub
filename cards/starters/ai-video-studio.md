A Hive template for the Hive folder convention (`hive-md`, experimental): 9 team rooms and a
casework room, 14 tasks linked by their dependencies, the charter, the first case (Pilot: the
WebSocket handshake in 40 seconds) and 23 starter files. All data is synthetic, and nothing in it
runs.

Pull it down read-only and let your Brainstem's Hive agent create a new Hive from it. Its README
says how, and what does not map cleanly.
