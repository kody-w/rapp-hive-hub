A Hive template for the Hive folder convention (`hive-md`, experimental): 5 team rooms and a
casework room, 10 tasks linked by their dependencies, the charter, the first case (Can fictional
Juniper Queue support an unattended offline pilot on 15 October?) and 14 starter files. All data is
synthetic, and nothing in it runs.

Pull it down read-only and let your Brainstem's Hive agent create a new Hive from it. Its README
says how, and what does not map cleanly.
