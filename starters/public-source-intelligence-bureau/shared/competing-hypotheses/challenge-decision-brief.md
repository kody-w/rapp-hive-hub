# Challenge the brief's strongest and weakest conclusions

Room `competing-hypotheses` (hypothesis-reviewer). Blocked until [[draft-decision-brief]], [[plan-next-collection]] are done.

Attempt to explain the evidence under each alternative hypothesis. Identify overclaims, correlated corroboration, missing measurements, and what new evidence would reverse the recommendation.

- `deliverables/pilot-decision-brief.md`, from [[draft-decision-brief]]

## Outputs
