# The Applied Invention Lab

Test a practical packing idea before claiming an invention works.

Coordinate research, experiments, prototyping, replication, and commercialization around reproducible synthetic library-kit tote packing, with a runnable baseline and explicit limits on physical and business claims.

## Rooms

- `shared/research/`: Problem framing and interpretation. Define the practical question, assumptions, decision criteria, and honest limits of the scalar model.
- `shared/experiments/`: Computational experiments. Run the baseline and seeded comparisons without cherry-picking favorable scenarios.
- `shared/prototyping/`: Usable prototype and observation design. Turn computed assignments into reviewable packing instructions and a later physical-fit study.
- `shared/replication/`: Independent reproducibility. Audit the original dataset and reproduce checks, hashes, assignments, and limits.
- `shared/commercialization/`: Application and adoption assessment. Prepare a bounded opportunity hypothesis without fabricated demand, patents, savings, or customers.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[library-kit-tote-study]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `one-person-conglomerate`, `enterprise-transformation-firm`.

All names, estimates and observations are synthetic public starter data.
