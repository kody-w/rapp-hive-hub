# Prepare a harmless physical-fit observation protocol

Room `prototyping` (Usable prototype and observation design). Blocked until [[research-interpretation]], [[replication-run]] are done.

Design a later owner-approved study using empty or lightweight mock materials, not real transport or lifting limits. Specify geometry, item accessibility, labeling, and operator-comprehension observations without recording invented results.

## Inputs

- `deliverables/research-interpretation.md`, from [[research-interpretation]]
- `deliverables/replication-report.json`, from [[replication-run]]
- `deliverables/packing-instructions.html`, from [[packing-prototype]]
- `docs/prototype-brief.md`: [[prototype-brief]]
- `docs/operator-worksheet.csv`: [[operator-worksheet.csv]]

## Outputs

- `deliverables/physical-fit-protocol.csv`

## Acceptance

- The protocol distinguishes planned observation from an actual measured value.
- Mock geometry and readability checks have explicit pass/fail criteria.
- No lifting-safety, structural-load, shipping, or real-world fit guarantee is proposed from scalar units.
