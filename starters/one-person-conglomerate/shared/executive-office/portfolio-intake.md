# Open the five-unit portfolio intake

Room `executive-office` (Portfolio owner). Ready to claim: it depends on nothing.

Create one row per business unit with its proposed job, owner workspace, evidence gap, and explicit non-goals. Preserve the synthetic labels and protected reserves. Do not activate memberships or authorize spending.

## Inputs

- `data/portfolio.csv`: [[portfolio.csv]]
- `docs/charter.md`: [[charter]]
- `data/capacity.json`: [[capacity.json]]

## Outputs

- `deliverables/portfolio-intake.csv`

## Acceptance

- Exactly five distinct business units are represented.
- The intake names 8 reserved hours and USD 100 reserved cash.
- No row represents revenue, customers, or approval as established.
