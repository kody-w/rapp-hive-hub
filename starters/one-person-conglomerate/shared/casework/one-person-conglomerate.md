# The One-Person Conglomerate

Five small businesses, one explicitly limited founder.

Allocate a synthetic founder's attention across five distinct business units using bounded learning experiments, shared operating functions, and evidence rather than invented revenue.

## Rooms

- `shared/executive-office/`: Portfolio owner. Own capacity limits, decisions, and stop conditions without confusing a recommendation with authority.
- `shared/portfolio-research/`: Shared research. Separate assumptions, public desk research, and later consented field evidence.
- `shared/portfolio-finance/`: Shared finance. Maintain the founder-hour and hypothetical cash worksheet; no profit forecasts masquerade as facts.
- `shared/platform-engineering/`: Shared engineering. Maintain the offline allocation analysis and reusable CSV preflight reference tool.
- `shared/portfolio-go-to-market/`: Shared go-to-market. Draft differentiated offers and approval-gated learning invitations for each unit.
- `shared/ledgerleaf-tools/`: Business unit: file utilities. Explore small offline CSV quality tools for people preparing imports.
- `shared/fieldnote-guides/`: Business unit: practical guides. Develop original, non-safety-critical workspace organization guides.
- `shared/quietbench-templates/`: Business unit: accessible templates. Prototype accessible meeting and purchasing-request templates.
- `shared/routepaper-planners/`: Business unit: printable planners. Explore printable errand grouping sheets without collecting real location histories.
- `shared/tinylesson-studio/`: Business unit: short lessons. Create original offline lessons on basic file organization and data hygiene.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[first-portfolio-cycle]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `product-launch-company`, `applied-invention-lab`.

All names, estimates and observations are synthetic public starter data.
