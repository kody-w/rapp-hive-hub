# The Turnaround Firm

Join cash diagnosis to a bounded, testable recovery sprint.

Bring finance, product recovery, engineering, operations, and customer success together around a distressed fictional subscription business. Reconcile the original synthetic financials, reproduce a real small dispatch defect, and plan a constrained recovery sprint without claiming realized savings or customer outcomes.

## Rooms

- `shared/finance/`: turnaround-finance-lead. Reconcile cash, reserve unpaid obligations, and separate scenario arithmetic from realized results.
- `shared/product-recovery/`: recovery-product-lead. Connect support impact, scope, priority rules, and the final hold/rework decision.
- `shared/engineering/`: recovery-engineer. Reproduce and repair the authored priority-sorting defect with narrow regression coverage.
- `shared/operations/`: recovery-operations-lead. Create capacity-bounded support work, validate handoff behavior, and retain blocked/deferred work.
- `shared/customer-success/`: customer-recovery-lead. Prepare truthful, approval-gated response drafts and avoid unearned commitments or fabricated resolution claims.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[cedarline-five-day-recovery]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `enterprise-transformation-firm`, `product-launch-company`.

All names, estimates and observations are synthetic public starter data.
