# The Enterprise Transformation Firm

Turn a concrete quote-to-cash exception queue into a reviewable pilot.

Coordinate account strategy, discovery, architecture, engineering, quality, and adoption around an original synthetic enterprise process and an executable offline calculation boundary.

## Rooms

- `shared/account-strategy/`: Engagement strategy. Define the fictional engagement's value hypothesis, sponsor decisions, exclusions, and pilot gates.
- `shared/discovery/`: Process and requirements discovery. Reconcile source facts, exceptions, field definitions, and traceable requirements.
- `shared/architecture/`: Solution architecture. Define a replaceable pure calculation boundary and human-owned exception routing.
- `shared/engineering/`: Prototype engineering. Reproduce the offline quote calculation and propose evidence-backed data corrections.
- `shared/quality/`: Acceptance and quality. Check exact decimal arithmetic, readiness classifications, data quality, and release evidence.
- `shared/adoption/`: Operational adoption. Prepare role-specific practice, exception ownership, and reversible pilot handoff.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[lattice-harbor-quote-pilot]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `product-launch-company`, `open-source-infrastructure-foundation`.

All names, estimates and observations are synthetic public starter data.
