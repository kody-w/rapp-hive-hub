# Prepare a minimal help and reproduction kit

Room `support` (Support and product learning). Blocked until [[design-chat]] is done.

Write help and failure/restart guidance consistent with the proposed product. Define consent-aware intake using synthetic reproductions and minimum necessary data. Do not promise a staffed service.

## Inputs

- `deliverables/product-spec.md`, from [[specify-product]]
- `deliverables/chat-design.md`, from [[design-chat]]
- `docs/support-playbook.md`: [[support-playbook]]
- `templates/feedback.json`: [[feedback.json]]

## Outputs

- `deliverables/support-kit.md`

## Acceptance

- Unknown commands, duplicate IDs, limits and session loss have actionable explanations.
- No credential, full transcript, real checklist or private path is required by default.
- Any service level, retention or outreach commitment awaits owner approval.
