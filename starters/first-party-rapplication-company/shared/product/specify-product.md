# Specify the smallest useful checklist product

Room `product` (Product scope and acceptance). Blocked until [[configure-company]] is done.

Turn the synthetic proposal into a reviewable product boundary. Select the intended authorized chat host or record it as unavailable; state adapter work, exclusions, acceptance, budgets and a disconfirmation criterion. Do not claim research or runtime access.
