# The First-Party Rapplication Company

Craft chat-operated products; earn every internal and public release.

Design, build, internally release, dogfood, critique, and iterate small first-party rapplications. Promote only independently verified, separately approved, sanitized releases through public pull requests merged by the owner.

## Rooms

- `shared/ceo-office/`: Founder-CEO office. Own focus, configurable persona, mission and evidence-specific craftsmanship critique. Recommend, never self-approve or merge.
- `shared/product/`: Product scope and acceptance. Own the useful outcome, exclusions, acceptance criteria, and bounded iteration plan without inventing user validation.
- `shared/design/`: Conversation and interaction design. Own clear chat journeys, error recovery, copy and observed accessibility of the intended host.
- `shared/engineering/`: Rapplication engineering. Build a small reproducible candidate with explicit dependencies, tests and exact artifact bindings; never act as its independent verifier.
- `shared/quality/`: Independent quality and verification. Reproduce actual behavior, inspect hidden details, and verify exact candidates and public projections with identities distinct from builders and the CEO.
- `shared/release/`: Internal release and public promotion. Prepare authorized internal distributions, request separate external decisions, and hand sanitized checked PRs to the owner without self-granting publication or merge authority.
- `shared/support/`: Support and product learning. Maintain honest help and consent-aware minimal feedback, prefer synthetic reproduction, and retain unresolved product observations.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[session-checklist-first-release]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `product-launch-company`, `open-source-infrastructure-foundation`.

All names, estimates and observations are synthetic public starter data.
