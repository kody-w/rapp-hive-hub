# The Product Launch Company

Launch a real offline agenda planner without inventing traction.

Coordinate product strategy, design, engineering, creative, distribution, and customer success around Agenda Pocket, an original usable local demo with explicit acceptance and approval-gated launch work.

## Rooms

- `shared/product-strategy/`: Product strategy. Own the problem hypothesis, scope, measurable acceptance, and conditional launch decision.
- `shared/design/`: Interaction and accessibility design. Make time-budget tradeoffs understandable and test keyboard, reading order, and error recovery.
- `shared/engineering/`: Offline product engineering. Maintain the runnable demo, deterministic agenda core, and reproducible release evidence.
- `shared/creative/`: Product storytelling. Prepare accurate copy and a clearly labeled storyboard without pretending media was generated.
- `shared/distribution/`: Launch operations. Plan approved channels, reversible publication, and an evidence-linked launch runbook.
- `shared/customer-success/`: Support and learning. Prepare practical help, a local-data boundary, and a consent-aware feedback instrument.
- `shared/casework/`: the shared delivery case. Its intake is [[agenda-pocket-first-launch]]; shared inputs sit in `artifacts/`; accepted deliverables land here too.

Related starters, for discovery only (never shared membership): `one-person-conglomerate`, `open-source-infrastructure-foundation`.
