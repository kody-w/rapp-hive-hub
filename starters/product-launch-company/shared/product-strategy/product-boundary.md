# Approve a proposed product boundary for review

Room `product-strategy` (Product strategy). Ready to claim: it depends on nothing.

Refine the supplied problem hypothesis into a decision brief covering who might benefit, what the local demo actually does, what it excludes, and what would disconfirm usefulness. Do not claim user validation.

## Inputs

- `docs/product-brief.md`: [[product-brief]]
- `data/sample-agenda.json`: [[sample-agenda.json]]

## Outputs

## Acceptance

- Time budgeting is separate from calendar scheduling and meeting facilitation.
- The brief preserves the synthetic nature of the sample.
- A measurable disconfirmation criterion and owner approval boundary are included.
